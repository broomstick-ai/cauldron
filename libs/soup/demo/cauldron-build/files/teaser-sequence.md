# The teaser, shot by shot

1. Open cauldron.
2. Name a new environment. It starts empty.
3. Add a cluster, with its boundary in your own words.
4. Type in the bucket and dump.
5. The dump appears in the ledger.
6. Switch to the cauldron-build environment.
7. Open a cluster.
8. Browse its questions and answer one through the bucket.
9. Go to Actions and press one: a multi-phase action with a masked step.
10. Show Threads.
